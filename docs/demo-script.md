# Demo video script

Target length: 100 seconds. The submission form asks for 1 to 2 minutes. Aspect 16:9, 1440x900 capture at device scale 2.

The video has one job: show that shared wording is not a dependency, and that a person decides. Everything else is support.

## Recording notes

- Record against the running app at `http://127.0.0.1:4173`, not a mockup.
- Type with human timing (80 to 150 ms per key) and ease the pointer; do not teleport the cursor.
- Reset the working store before the take so the queue is identical every time: delete `.work/workbench.rl`.
- One take per shot, then pick the cleanest. Do not record in one continuous pass.
- Burned captions, not a voiceover, unless a voice track is recorded separately.
- Keep the pointer out of the trace and quote areas during shots 2 and 3; they are the argument.

## What the video must not do

- Not claim automatic detection. The reviewer sets every direction.
- Not show a document pack larger than the fixtures, or imply a benchmark.
- Not show the app catching a real company's documents.
